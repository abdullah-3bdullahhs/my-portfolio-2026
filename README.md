# ABDULLAH HASSAN JAMAL — INTERACTIVE PORTFOLIO (PHP Edition 2026)

## Architecture & Features
- **Official Brand Logo**: User's signature `3bdullah.hs` wordmark integrated into header, loader, and mobile panel.
- **Dynamic Animated Studio Lighting**: Smooth couple-color transitions across each slide (Home: Orange & Violet, Work: Cyan & Pink, About: Emerald & Purple, Services: Amber & Blue, Contact: Crimson & Indigo) powered by GPU-accelerated CSS `@property` custom variables.
- **Hero Center Badge**: Badge `ABDULLAH PORTFOLIO · 2026` precisely centered in the middle of concentric orbital rings.
- **Academic & Focus Bio**: "Student at Jordan University of Science and Technology · B.Sc. of Digital Film and Multimedia Technology".
- **Real Works Archive**: 23 original works integrated with folder & file-matched nomenclature across Branding, Advertising, Digital, and Academic categories with zero repetition.
- **Visual Experiments Layout**: Smaller distance (tight 10px spacing), medium-sized balanced spans, and native uncropped official aspect ratio display.
- **Default Category**: Initial view loads "BRANDING" by default (not "ALL"). The "ALL" filter remains available on demand.
- **Services Showcase**: Tailored service cards with high-quality visible photos.
- **Performance Optimized**: Instant load time, lazy-loaded media, and lightweight video modal previews.

## How to Run Locally (PC & Phone)
1. Double-click `run_local.bat`
2. **On your PC**: Open [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)
3. **On your Phone / Tablet (same Wi-Fi)**: Open the network link displayed in the terminal (e.g., `http://192.168.0.101:8000`)

## How to Host 24/7 (InfinityFree or any PHP Web Host)
Upload `index.php`, `data.php`, `style.css`, `script.js`, and the `assets/` folder directly into your hosting `htdocs` directory.
